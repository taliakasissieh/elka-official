01 · About ELKA

A legacy in
every detail.

Founded in 1985, ELKA has spent more than four decades shaping refined living spaces across Jordan. We create bespoke kitchens, furniture and complete interior solutions where function and beauty feel inseparable.

Every space begins with listening. From measurement and design to manufacturing and installation, each detail is considered around the people who will live with it every day.
