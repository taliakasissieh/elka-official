About ELKA

A legacy in
every detail.

Founded in 1985, ELKA has spent more than four decades shaping refined living spaces in Jordan. From kitchens and wardrobes to complete interior solutions, every project is approached as a balance of function, proportion, material and craftsmanship.

Our process moves from consultation and site measurement through design, manufacturing and installation—creating spaces tailored to the people who live in them.
